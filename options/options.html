Rad-Copilot — Template Library & Settings

Templates

    Settings

    Everything runs on your computer. No findings or reports are stored or transmitted. Draft persistence is intentionally disabled (health-data minimisation). The local whisper server option requires running the free, open-source whisper.cpp server on the same machine (see README).

    Templates are plain JSON: sections[].normalStatements hold your organ normal patterns, slots map which normal line a documented finding replaces, and rules define spoken phrases → professional sentences (placeholders like {sizePhrase}, {sitePhrase}, {qualifierPhrase}).
